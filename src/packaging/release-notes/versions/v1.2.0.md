- **A cleaner, calmer interface.** A new **Modern** look (flat, no particle
  background) is the default; the old glassy look is still there as **Legacy**
  under Settings > Appearance.
- **A tidier Settings window.** Everything is in one place, in clear sections:
  Personal, Model, Appearance, Voice & pictures and About. One **Save** button
  saves all of it (before, there were two, and each saved only half).
- **Plain-language model settings.** Creativity is now a "Focused to Creative"
  slider, answer length and conversation memory explain what they do, and
  **Reset to recommended** puts them back.
- **Chats name themselves.** After the first answer, HyperFlux gives the chat a
  short title instead of using the first few words of your question.
- **A clearer sidebar.** Chats are grouped by Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days
  and Older, the open chat is highlighted, and the newest chat moves to the top.
  **Ctrl+K** jumps to the chat search, and the sidebar can be hidden.
- **A quieter home page:** a greeting, the message box, four quick starts
  (Explain, Write code, Search the web, Ask about a picture), the patch notes
  and the feature spotlight side by side.
- **Easier reading.** Answers sit in one centred column at a comfortable width,
  and saved chats open instantly instead of fading in again.
- **One calm "thinking" animation** instead of a pulsing dot and bouncing dots,
  and it no longer restarts every time the answer box updates.
- **Scrollbars now match your theme** instead of being white.
- **Read aloud:** natural Windows voices are listed first, there is a speed
  slider, and a **Play** button to try a voice before choosing it.
- **Sources:** an answer never shows two source lists, and sources are listed
  only when the answer actually cites them.
- **Deleting all chats now asks first**, and shows how many will be deleted.
- **All three models write better.** Flash 1 leads with the fact and drops the
  preamble; Apex 1 gives concrete specifics, a clear recommendation when there is
  a choice, and the reasoning that matters on hard questions; Horizon 1 writes
  with more craft on long-form and creative work.
- **Other models work better.** HyperFlux is written for Gemma 4, but if you load
  another GGUF (Qwen, GLM, DeepSeek R1, Llama...), its thinking tags and chat
  tokens are now understood instead of showing up as raw text, and the model
  picker says when the loaded model isn't Gemma 4.
- **Thinking adapts to the model you load.** Gemma 4 keeps its built-in thinking;
  other models are asked in plain words to think (or not to), and Qwen and GLM
  are switched with their own `/no_think` setting. Gemma's thinking markers are
  no longer sent to models that don't understand them.
