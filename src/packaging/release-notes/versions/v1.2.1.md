- **Search answers repeat themselves less.** HyperFlux now removes repeated
  facts from the web pages before the model reads them, tells the model to say
  each fact once, and merges repeated sentences in the answer (keeping every
  citation).
- **Fixed: the repetition setting was never applied.** HyperFlux sent it under a
  name the model server ignores, so answers could loop or repeat. It now works.
- **Other models work better.** HyperFlux is written for Gemma 4, but if you load
  another GGUF (Qwen, GLM, DeepSeek R1, Llama...), its thinking tags and chat
  tokens are understood instead of showing up as raw text, and the model picker
  says when the loaded model isn't Gemma 4.
- **Thinking adapts to the model you load.** Gemma 4 keeps its built-in thinking;
  other models are asked in plain words to think (or not to), and Qwen and GLM
  are switched with their own `/no_think` setting. Gemma's thinking markers are
  no longer sent to models that don't understand them.
- **All three models write better.** Flash 1 leads with the fact and drops the
  preamble; Apex 1 gives concrete specifics, a clear recommendation when there is
  a choice, and the reasoning that matters on hard questions; Horizon 1 writes
  with more craft on long-form and creative work.
